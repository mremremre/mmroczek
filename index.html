About

I'm an independent AI Safety researcher from Poland with a background in theoretical mathematics. Currently in the MATS program under Richard Ngo's stream, following a Dovetail Research Fellowship where I worked on Internal Model Principle. Before pivoting to AI Safety, I spent several years as a Data Scientist and AI Engineer at Samsung R&D Poland.

I hold an MSc in Pure Mathematics from the Jagiellonian University in Kraków, where I'm also pursuing a PhD in mathematics, with research on ergodic theory and dynamical systems for amenable group actions.
